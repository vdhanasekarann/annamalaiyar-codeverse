If we offer a free trial or pilot, you may cancel before the end of the trial period and no subscription charge will be made. Once the trial converts into a paid plan, normal billing and refund rules apply.

Plan upgrades may take effect immediately or from the next billing cycle, depending on the arrangement. Downgrades will normally apply from the next renewal date. We do not usually refund the difference for the current billing period when downgrading.

In such cases, refunds may or may not be provided, at our sole discretion, except where a refund is mandated by applicable law.

Fees for one-time services (for example, WhatsApp bot setup, custom workflow design, consulting or training) are normally non-refundable once the work has started, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email dhanasekar@kannizconites.com with:

We will review your request and respond within 2 business days. Approval of any refund is at our sole discretion, subject to applicable law.
