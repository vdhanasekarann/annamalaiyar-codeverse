# Google Play App Access (Reviewer Credentials)

Use this in Play Console > App content > App access.

## App access type
- `All or some functionality is restricted`

## Instructions for reviewer (English)
- Open reviewer URL: `https://app.aicodeverse.com/login?play_review=1`.
- On the login screen, use **Google Play Review Access**.
- Tap **Continue with Demo Account**.
- No password is required.

## Notes
- Demo account is always available and reusable.
- Access works regardless of location.
- No OTP, QR code, or invitation code required.
